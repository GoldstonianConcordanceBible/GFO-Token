# $WAR DAO Doctrine

## Eat What You Kill: The Goldstonian Doctrine

The $WAR DAO is built on contribution, not entitlement.

The doctrine is simple:

```text
Eat what you kill.
Document what you build.
Receive what you prove.

Meaning

This does not refer to violence.

It means:

* You earn from what you contribute.
* You receive from what you document.
* You gain standing from what you build.
* You do not extract from work you did not do.
* You do not claim credit for witness you did not carry.

The DAO Is a Ledger of Witness

The $WAR DAO should function as:

* A contribution ledger

* A cultural reconstruction system
