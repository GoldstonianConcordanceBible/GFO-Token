Purpose

This folder contains the complete research methodology for the Global Social Experiment.

Unlike a traditional research project that produces a single paper, this repository is designed as a living, version-controlled research system in which methods, evidence, governance decisions, and publications evolve transparently over time.

The methodology documents:

* how research questions were developed;
* how evidence is collected and classified;
* how AI models participate as structured reviewers;
* how consensus and dissent are preserved;
* how legal claims are verified;
* how repository changes are governed;
* and how findings move into books, articles, conference presentations, websites, and consulting deliverables.

Research Philosophy

The Global Social Experiment combines multiple methodological traditions rather than relying on a single research paradigm.

Candidate influences include:

* design science research;
* mixed methods research;
* grounded theory;
* documentary analysis;
* systems thinking;
* repository-based governance;
* Delphi methodology;
* AI-assisted qualitative synthesis;
* comparative policy analysis;
* and implementation science.

No single tradition is treated as sufficient by itself.
