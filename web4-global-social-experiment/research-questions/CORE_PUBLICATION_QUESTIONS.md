Scope-Control Checklist

Before publication, confirm:

* Does the output answer its declared questions?
* Are unresolved questions still labeled unresolved?
* Are legal claims verified?
* Are institutional relationships accurately described?
* Are theology and mythology clearly labeled?
* Are model outputs distinguished from evidence?
* Are hypothetical cases labeled?
* Is the repository version identified?
* Are limitations included?
* Are commercial offerings separated from research results?

⸻

Canonical Principle

The same repository may support several outputs, but each output has a different scholarly and public purpose.

The governing rule is:

one-canonical-question-bank
→ narrower-output-question-set
→ evidence-mapped-claims
→ explicit-limitations
→ versioned-publication
→ correction-tracking

The strength of the project depends not on answering every question at once, but on selecting the right questions for each output and answering only what the available evidence can support.
