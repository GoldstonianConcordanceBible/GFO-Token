1. Purpose

This file contains the first twenty-five secondary research questions in the Global Social Experiment research agenda.

It covers two related domains:

1. the foundations, scope, boundaries, and research design of the Global Social Experiment; and
2. the emergence, definition, minimum properties, competing explanations, and falsifiability of Web4.

These questions establish the conceptual foundation for every later repository domain.

Questions involving Web4 NIL, DAO governance, compensation, legal verification, privacy, education, theology, healthcare, and implementation depend on the definitions and boundaries developed here.

Short Title

Falsification of the Research Program

Purpose

This question prevents the research program from becoming unfalsifiable or purely promotional.

* failed replication;
* legal changes;
* technological changes;
* implementation failures;
* or stronger competing theories.

Canonical Principle

The Global Social Experiment and Web4 must remain distinguishable from branding, project activity, and model-generated language.

The research program should be capable of showing:

* where the concepts originated;
* which properties were observed;
* which properties were interpreted;
* which claims remain hypothetical;
* what evidence would strengthen them;
* and what evidence would require revision or rejection.
